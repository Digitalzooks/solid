The SOLID 100

An annual recognition of one hundred Nigerian men whose work, character and influence are setting the standard for a generation.

About the recognition

The SOLID 100 is not a rich list and it is not a popularity contest. It is a considered record of men doing serious work: building companies, healing patients, teaching children, making art and serving the public with distinction.

Each year our editors, guided by an independent advisory panel, select one hundred honourees across ten categories. The list is published in full, with a profile of every man on it.

Recognition categories

  1. 01Business & Enterprise
  2. 02Technology
  3. 03Finance & Investment
  4. 04Public Service
  5. 05Health & Science
  6. 06Education & Mentorship
  7. 07Arts & Culture
  8. 08Media & Entertainment
  9. 09Sports
  10. 10Philanthropy & Impact

Selection criteria

1

Achievement

A sustained record of excellence in his field, measured in outcomes rather than attention.

2

Character

A reputation for integrity among the people who know his work best.

3

Influence

Evidence that he raises the standard for those around him and those coming after.

4

Impact

A contribution that reaches beyond personal success to community and country.
