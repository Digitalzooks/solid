SOLID MAG — logo kit
Traced from "Concept 3 — Masthead". All SVG files are vector and scale to any size.

FILES
  solid-mag-logo-*      Primary logo: THE / SOLID MAG / rule / tagline
  solid-mag-wordmark-*  Compact wordmark: SOLID MAG only (site header, small spaces)
  sm-mark-*             SM monogram (favicon, app icon, social avatar)
  favicon/              Ready-made favicon and app-icon sizes

COLOURWAYS
  -light   Black and red, for white or light backgrounds
  -dark    White and red, for black or dark backgrounds
  -white   All white, for brand red backgrounds and photography
  -black   All black, for one-colour print and stamps

COLOURS
  Solid Red   #C80F2E   RGB 200 15 46
  Ink         #141414   RGB 20 20 20
  White       #FFFFFF

SIZING
  Primary logo: use at 480px wide or more; below that the tagline is too small to read.
  Wordmark: down to about 100px wide.
  SM mark: down to 16px (favicon).
  Clear space: keep at least the height of the "S" in SOLID free around the logo.
